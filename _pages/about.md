# Bio

Hello and welcome to my website! I’m a Computer Science researcher currently pursuing my Ph.D. at North Carolina State University, under the guidance of Dr. [Man-Ki Yoon](https://mankiyoon.github.io). My current research passions lie in autonomous systems and robotics.

My research sits at the intersection of robotics and systems — focused on building autonomous systems that remain accountable, reliable and safe even under adversarial attacks or unpredictable real-world conditions.

Before pursuing my Ph.D., I worked at [KPIT](https://www.kpit.com) where I developed ADAS applications for a top Japanese automotive company. I also interned at [Analog Devices](https://www.analog.com/en/index.html) where I was involved in device driver development for ADI’s next-gen Digital Signal Processors (DSPs). I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT), Manipal.
