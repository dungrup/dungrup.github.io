## Teaching Assistant

<div class="teaching-entry">
  <h3>Self-Driving Cars: Theory and Practice</h3>
  <p class="teaching-meta"><strong>Department of Computer Science, NC State</strong> &nbsp;|&nbsp; Springs 2024 - 2026</p>

  <p>As a Teaching Assistant for CSC 491/591: Self-Driving Cars: Theory and Practice, I was involved in implementing a comprehensive curriculum that equipped students with the fundamental skills required to build autonomous robots. Through a hands-on approach, we guided students in mastering technologies such as ROS2 (Robot Operating System 2), computer vision, deep learning, and control and path planning techniques. The course culminated in a capstone project where students applied their knowledge to develop a 1:16 scale fully autonomous vehicle, with features including:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>Fully autonomous building-to-building navigation at NC State Centennial Campus</li>
    <li>Color based object sorting</li>
    <li>Stop sign detection and actuation</li>
    <li>CV based autonomous indoor track navigation</li>
  </ul>

<div class="teaching-entry">
  <h3>Software for Robotics</h3>
  <p class="teaching-meta"><strong>Department of Computer Science, NC State</strong> &nbsp;|&nbsp; Spring 2026</p>

  <p>I served as a teaching assistant for CSC 491/591: Software for Robotics, which introduces the fundamental software tools used in modern robotics, including ROS2, ROS2 Bags, Docker, URDF, visualization tools such as Foxglove, and simulation environments such as Gazebo. The course also included a research paper reading component where seminal works in robotics were discussed in depth. The capstone objective was for students to design and develop a robotic system that acquires sensor data, processes it, and performs actuation. A wide variety of projects were developed, primarily in simulation:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>Evaluating UGV and UGV + UAV systems for disaster management and rescue</li>
    <li>Creating highly realistic 3D environment maps using Gaussian Splatting</li>
    <li>Building a Reinforcement Learning gym in CARLA to improve autonomous vehicle behavior</li>
    <li>Developing a multi-robot system for efficient environment navigation and mapping</li>
    <li>Building a ROS2-based framework for autonomous drone operation</li>
  </ul>

  <p>My primary role was mentoring and guiding students through their projects and leading workshops conducted as part of the course.</p>
</div>

  <h3>Operating Systems Design</h3>
  <p class="teaching-meta"><strong>Department of Computer Science, NC State</strong> &nbsp;|&nbsp; Fall 2024 - 2025</p>

  <p>I served as a teaching assistant for both the undergraduate (CSC 246) and graduate (CSC 501) sections of the Operating Systems course. The course covers core OS concepts including process management, system calls, virtual memory and paging, and inter-process communication. My responsibilities included designing and grading programming assigments that assessed students' understanding of these fundamentals.</p>

## Mentoring

<div class="teaching-entry">
  <h3>High School Research Experience — NCSSM</h3>
  <p class="teaching-meta"><strong>Department of Computer Science, NC State</strong> &nbsp;|&nbsp; Summers 2023–2025</p>

  <p>I mentored 8 high school students from the North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics (NCSSM) over three summers. The students developed applications relevant to autonomous robots, including:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>An autonomous "pet" robot capable of following gesture and voice commands to follow a user</li>
    <li>A low-latency streaming application for remote teleoperation of robots</li>
    <li>A LiDAR and IR sensor-based SLAM algorithm for indoor autonomous operation</li>
  </ul>
</div>
